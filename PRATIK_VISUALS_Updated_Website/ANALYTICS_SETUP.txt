ANALYTICS SETUP — GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4

Analytics is intentionally not active yet because a real GA4 Measurement ID was not provided.

To activate:
1. Create or open a Google Analytics 4 property.
2. In Admin > Data streams, open the website stream and copy its Measurement ID (format: G-XXXXXXXXXX).
3. Send that ID to the developer or insert the official Google tag in the <head> of index.html.
4. Deploy the site and verify events in GA4 Realtime.

Do not use a made-up ID. Avoid adding multiple copies of the Google tag.
